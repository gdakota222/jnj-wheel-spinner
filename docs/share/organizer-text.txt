Hi! Here is how to get the JnJ Wheel Spinner and use it. It is a spinning wheel for Jack & Jill partner draws: spin for a leader, a follower, and a challenge for the couple. No accounts, no ads, works offline once installed.

THE APP: https://gdakota222.github.io/jnj-wheel-spinner/

GET IT (do this once, on wifi)

ANDROID (Chrome)
1. Open Chrome and go to the link above.
2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
3. Tap "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen"), then Install.
4. Open it from the new icon and let it load while you are online.

IPHONE / IPAD (must be Safari)
1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow).
3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
4. Open it from the new icon and let it load while you are online.

After that it works with no signal. Your dancers and night are saved on that one device only.

RUN A NIGHT
1. Start a Session.
2. Add dancers, each as Leader, Follower or Switch (names need a last initial, like "Sarah M"). 5 couples is the sweet spot, 10 max.
3. Pick who is drawn first, and whether couples get a challenge.
4. Check the summary and start.
5. Tap the wheel to spin. Tap again to hurry it, double-tap to skip. Spin for the first dancer, the second, then the challenge.
6. While they dance, the screen holds. Press and HOLD "Next couple" when they are done.
7. At the end: Crown a Winner and/or AllSkate, or neither.

Handy: Undo (asks first), Tools (re-spin, redraw a challenge, add a late arrival), Lock (hold to unlock). Nobody sits out and no couple repeats. You can hand the device to someone else and it carries on where it was.

Stuck? "How it works" on the title screen explains every feature. If the app looks old or will not open, connect to wifi and open it twice. On iPhone, no "Add to Home Screen" usually means you are not in Safari.
