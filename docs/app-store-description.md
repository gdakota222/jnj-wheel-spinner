# JnJ Wheel Spinner — App Store Description

**Status:** Draft copy
**Last updated:** 2026-08-09
**Source:** Written from [intent.md](intent.md)

## Description

Turn the partner draw into the best moment of the night. JnJ Wheel Spinner takes the
pairing that usually happens on a clipboard and puts it on a big, bright, spinning wheel
the whole room can watch — spin for a leader, spin for a follower, and let the cheer
happen. Add your dancers once, mark them as leaders, followers, or switches, and the app
handles the rest: everyone dances, nobody sits out, and no couple repeats. Flip on the
challenge wheel and every pair gets a named dare — *A Whole New Level*, anyone? — drawn
fresh so nothing repeats all night. Then crown a winner your way, by your call or by
passing the tablet for the crowd to vote, behind a theatre curtain that keeps the secret
until the countdown drops. No accounts, no setup, no internet required — because venue
wifi always dies right when you need it.

## Notes on the copy

Two deliberate choices, recorded so they survive future edits:

- **The sloth theme is not mentioned, and should never be.** A store listing is the one
  place a hidden easter egg cannot appear without ceasing to be one. See the Sloth theme
  section of [intent.md](intent.md).
- **Every claim is one the product actually makes.** "Nobody sits out" is the recycling
  behavior, "no couple repeats" is guaranteed structurally, and "no internet required" is
  a real design constraint — not marketing license. If the build ever changes any of
  these, this copy has to change with it.

Claims to keep out unless the product changes: multiple rounds per night, saved or
exportable results, dancers voting from their own phones, and any dance style beyond
West Coast Swing.
